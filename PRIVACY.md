# Privacy

MilkDrop TV processes audio on the Android TV device.
The app does not transmit audio, presets, settings, or crash logs.

## Audio

System Audio uses the Android playback-capture interface.
Android displays a consent prompt before capture starts.
Some playback apps do not permit capture.

Microphone mode requires microphone permission.
The app processes microphone samples in memory and does not save them.

## Crash Logs

The app stores crash logs in its private app storage.
The user can inspect, clear, or share a crash log from Settings.
The app does not send crash logs automatically.

## Storage

The app stores extracted presets, imported presets, favorites, and settings locally.
Uninstall the app to remove this data.
